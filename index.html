Goals

Three at most, on a 12-week horizon — long enough to matter, short enough that "there's plenty of time" is obviously false. Each goal carries a lead measure (what you do weekly, which you control) and a lag measure (the outcome, which you don't). Tracking only the outcome tells you you've failed after it's too late.
